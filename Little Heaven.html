Welcome to Little Haven!

We are a small business that specializes in pastry and desserts.
Perfect shop for those who had long days or feeling a sugary craving
or just want to treat yourself with a sweet treat.
Welcome to Little Haven, a cozy café created to bring people together over great coffee, delicious food, and warm hospitality.
We believe that every cup tells a story, which is why we carefully select quality ingredients and prepare every drink and meal with passion and attention to detail.
From rich, aromatic coffees and refreshing handcrafted beverages to freshly baked pastries and satisfying light meals, there's something for everyone to enjoy.
Whether you're looking for a peaceful corner to read, a comfortable space to work, or the perfect spot to catch up with friends and family, Little Haven offers a relaxing atmosphere where you can slow down and enjoy life's simple moments.
Our friendly team is dedicated to making every visit memorable, ensuring that each guest feels welcomed from the moment they walk through our doors.
Explore our menu to discover customer favorites and seasonal specials, all made with fresh ingredients and inspired by the joy of sharing good food and drinks. At Little Haven, we're more than just a café—we're a place where conversations begin, ideas come to life, and lasting memories are made. We look forward to welcoming you and making every visit feel like coming home.
